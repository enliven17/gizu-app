### Why the vault deposit left 2.464035 USDC

[Circle's verified TokenPaymasterV08](https://eth.blockscout.com/address/0xdC57D57552256Df09FFEBED067bAA06eE49415f1) takes a maximum USDC prefund during UserOperation validation and refunds the difference after execution. Its `FeeLib.calculateTokenCost` computes `floor((gasCostWei * nativeTokenPrice) / 10^18) + 1` USDC atoms. The 1 atom is its explicit round-up. The paymaster also adds `additionalGasCharge` gas units and any configured fee spread. For this transaction the spread was zero, as shown by the `UserOperationSponsored` event's `feeTokenAmount = 0`.

The signed operation set gas limits of 906,026 (call), 61,585 (verification), 83,360 (pre-verification), 200,000 (paymaster verification), and 35,000 (paymaster post-operation): 1,285,971 total. Its maximum fee was 1,292,466,979 wei/gas. Circle added 35,000 gas units and used its event price of 2,652,033,376 USDC atoms per ETH. Thus its maximum prefund was `floor((1,285,971 + 35,000) × 1,292,466,979 × 2,652,033,376 / 10^18) + 1 = 4,527,847` USDC atoms.

The execution block's base fee was 370,104,562 wei/gas, so the UserOperation's effective fee was `min(1,292,466,979, 370,104,562 + 748,275,050) = 1,118,379,612` wei/gas. The `UserOperationSponsored` event reports an actual charge of 2,063,812 USDC atoms. Substituting that charge and the event price into Circle's formula yields **660,828 gas units before `postOp`** (uniquely at USDC's 6-decimal precision): `floor((660,828 + 35,000) × 1,118,379,612 × 2,652,033,376 / 10^18) + 1 = 2,063,812`. The EntryPoint receipt reports 672,955 total UserOperation gas units; its total includes execution after the value passed into Circle's `postOp`. Circle then refunded `4,527,847 − 2,063,812 = 2,464,035` USDC atoms to wallet 2.

#### Replaying the gas-limit effect

At the recorded token price, each 10,000 gas units added to any signed limit increase Circle's maximum prefund by about **0.034277 USDC** at the historical `maxFeePerGas`. The completed transaction's public [call trace](https://eth.blockscout.com/tx/0xe1216d7bcc7a27a26a14158f92498b17a9a45bbd972efaeaca7886df232f1992) shows 351,333 gas used inside the wallet execution call against its 906,026 limit, and 125,544 gas used by the paymaster validation call against its 200,000 limit. These inner-call figures exclude some EntryPoint overhead, so they are not safe replacement limits.

If the *final charge stayed at 2.063812 USDC*, a refund no greater than 0.1 USDC would require a prefund no greater than 2.163812 USDC. At the original maximum fee, that means the five signed gas limits must total at most **596,278** gas units. Even the sum of the traced wallet-call and paymaster-validation gas, the unchanged 83,360 pre-verification and 35,000 post-operation limits, and the 4,344-gas account validation call is **599,581**, before EntryPoint overhead or safety margin. Thus gas-limit reductions alone cannot reliably meet the target with that maximum fee bid.

At a hypothetical maximum fee equal to the historical effective price (1,118,379,612 wei/gas), the same fixed-charge calculation permits a signed-limit total of **694,543**. But the final charge is *not* fixed when the call limit is reduced: [EntryPoint v0.8](https://github.com/eth-infinitism/account-abstraction/blob/v0.8.0/contracts/core/EntryPoint.sol) adds a 10% penalty when unused execution gas exceeds 40,000. The large original call limit added approximately 53,682–55,469 penalty gas, based on the trace's bounds for execution gas. A tight call limit would remove that penalty, making the historical-path final Circle charge roughly **1.899293–1.904593 USDC**. With that lower charge and the hypothetical fee cap, a 0.1 USDC refund instead needs signed limits around **639,074–640,861** total. For example, limits of 365,000 call, 20,000 account verification, 83,360 pre-verification, 135,000 paymaster verification, and 35,000 post-operation total 638,360 and would prefund **1.997173 USDC** at that fee cap. This is arithmetic on the completed transaction, **not a verified safe gas configuration**: the call and paymaster margins are tight, the account-verification figure omits EntryPoint overhead, the fee cap has no room for a higher base fee, and a future deposit from wallet 2 follows a different EIP-7702 state path.

The runner does **not** use that hypothetical fixed fee. Each `vault-preview` or `vault-send` reads the latest Ethereum block, derives the exact next-block base fee, and adds the median 20th-percentile included priority fee from eight recent blocks. It stops before signing if the reference block changed during preparation. A send now checks Pimlico's live `slow` fee floor before signing because Pimlico refused the cheaper market bid during the 29 September test. For a signed-limit sum `L`, Circle's extra gas charge `A`, and token price `Q`, its approximate refund is `Q × [(L + A) × maxFeePerGas − (actualGasBeforePostOp + A) × min(maxFeePerGas, inclusionBlockBaseFee + maxPriorityFeePerGas)] / 10^18`, subject to integer rounding, spread and the execution-gas penalty. The inclusion block's base fee and actual gas are unknown before signing, so this bid cannot enforce the 0.1 USDC threshold. A 28 September 2026 read-only snapshot had a Pimlico `slow` maximum fee of 2,308,359,591 wei, tip of 1,095,623,089 wei, current base fee of 891,124,816 wei, and Circle price of 2,679,840,000 USDC atoms per ETH. At roughly 606,000 billable gas plus Circle's 35,000, the **fee-price gap alone** would refund about 0.552 USDC even if signed gas limits exactly matched actual gas.

The latest block's gas use makes the *next* block's base fee deterministic under [EIP-1559](https://eips.ethereum.org/EIPS/eip-1559). In that historical snapshot it was 878,794,567 wei. A hypothetical maximum fee of `nextBaseFee + quotedTip = 1,974,417,656` wei would remove the fee-price gap **if the operation were included in that next block**. Giving the bid room for one more maximum-size block raises it to about 2,084,266,976 wei and would itself create roughly 0.189 USDC of refund at the same gas use, before gas-limit slack. If inclusion misses the target block, run `vault-status 2` and then `vault-reprice 2`. Repricing prepares a fresh deposit and Circle permit using current balances, keeps the EntryPoint nonce, and saves both attempt hashes. It requires Pimlico's at-least-10% replacement bump for both fee fields; if a precise next-block bid cannot meet that bump or Pimlico's live minimum, it stops instead of adding fee-cap slack. The 29 September attempt established that Pimlico refused a market-priced bid. The 0.1 USDC goal remains conditional on a relay accepting a sufficiently tight fee and safe gas limits.

This calculation is reproducible after settlement. Before signing, the gas limits, fee bid, and current price can be read or estimated, but the gas consumed and effective fee in the inclusion block are not known exactly. A dynamic preview would need to simulate the complete operation, estimate Circle's final charge from that simulation, and then choose gas limits and a fee bid close enough to keep the expected refund below 0.1 USDC. A simulation is an estimate, so this threshold cannot be guaranteed with this paymaster. Do not lower the signed limits from this single historical sample: wallet 2 is now EIP-7702 delegated, so a later operation has a different execution path.

On 28 September, a **local Anvil fork** simulated wallet 2's current delegated state with a 0.1 USDC Morpho deposit. #### Current-state gas limit investigation

Circle's [verified TokenPaymasterV08](https://eth.blockscout.com/address/0xdC57D57552256Df09FFEBED067bAA06eE49415f1) pulls the maximum based on **all five signed limits**. Its `postOp` computes the final charge from the `actualGasCost` passed by [EntryPoint v0.8](https://github.com/eth-infinitism/account-abstraction/blob/v0.8.0/contracts/core/EntryPoint.sol), the effective fee, the configured `additionalGasCharge`, and any post-operation unused-gas penalty. With our 35,000 post-operation limit the Circle penalty is zero. EntryPoint's `actualGasCost` at that point includes the full `preVerificationGas` and any unused call-gas penalty, but excludes its later `postOp` execution. The emitted `UserOperationEvent.actualGasUsed` includes that later work, so substituting it directly into Circle's charge formula overstates the charge. The runner now has a tested `circleFinalCharge` function for this exact calculation; it also requires the observed USDC balance change to equal Circle's emitted `actualTokenNeeded` during status reconciliation.
Circle reads its token price during validation, so a preview made earlier is exact only while that price and fee settings stay unchanged. If they rise enough before inclusion, a permit set to the previewed maximum can fail rather than quietly pull more USDC.

## Recovery

`.local/state.json` persists quotes, exact signed UserOperations and intents, hashes, and progress with mode 0600. If a source broadcast is unresolved, run `status`, then `rebroadcast` to reuse its saved signed operation. If a payout submission is unresolved, run `status`, then `resubmit 1|2` to reuse its saved signed intent. If the vault deposit submission is unresolved or rejected by the bundler, run `vault-status 2`. `vault-resubmit 2` resends the exact saved operation only while its next-block bid is current; `vault-reprice 2` creates a same-nonce replacement and preserves prior hashes, including a rejected attempt. Repricing is a new signature and can increase Circle's maximum hold. It can fail when the 10% bundler replacement bump or Pimlico fee floor is incompatible with the next-block bid or wallet balance.

```sh
node --env-file=.env src/cli.mjs vault-reprice 2
```

If an unsigned funding quote expires or its source USDC gas estimate changes, run `requote`. If both payout quotes expire before either payout is signed, run `replan`. Do not start a new run until unresolved transactions have been reconciled. A payout is marked delivered only when Aurora reports success and Ethereum USDC transfer logs meet the quote minimum. The vault deposit is marked complete only after the UserOperation succeeds, the vault emits a matching Deposit event, wallet 2 receives shares, and the Circle fee reconciles within the signed cap.

The source paymaster test on Ethereum Sepolia remains available through `src/testnet-source.mjs` (`preview`, `send`, `status`). It tests only the source no-native-gas path, not Morpho deposits.
